# Walkthrough script ? English

A new tour built from nothing in CUE. 2:57.

## CUE — a new tour, built from nothing

`00_intro` ? frames: title card

This is not a tour of a demo. It is a new tour, built from nothing in CUE: a blank workspace, a real venue list, imported files, a design decision, an agent that finds the problem, and a tour that ends checked, priced, signed and ready.

## Six fields

`01_blank` ? frames: blank, new-form

We start with six fields: artist, title, tour, the first room and the date. No sample data underneath. Harbour Lights, The Long Way Round, Kade 12 in Amsterdam, in November.

## A valid workspace

`02_new` ? frames: tour-new

Create my tour gives a valid workspace — one venue, one look, one cue, no budget. The checklist is the path: venue, look, cue sheet, budget, date.

## Make it the real tour

`03_configure` ? frames: show-team, venue-form, date-form, venues-dates

Now make it the real tour. The team. A second room, The Foundry in Manchester: a twelve by eight stage, twenty-three kilowatts, a four-hundred-kilo rig rating. A second date. Two rooms with different limits — that is what a tour is.

## Real files

`04_imports` ? frames: imports, cues-imported

Instead of typing the rig, import the Vectorworks fixture schedule: twenty fixtures, twelve beams. Import the grandMA3 cue list: twelve cues. The files are parsed in the browser; nothing is uploaded. The blank placeholder cue is removed, so the sheet is the console's list.

## Build the look

`05_look` ? frames: look-form, studio-full

Build the look on the imported rig: all twenty fixtures, lime and cyan. The app computes twenty-seven point three kilowatts and four hundred and forty kilos on the front truss. This is the show the designer wants.

## The room has a limit

`06_checks` ? frames: checks-fail

Now the app does the work a production office does. Kade 12 can take it. The Foundry has twenty-three kilowatts and a four-hundred-kilo rating: the checks name the room, the limit and the gap — before anyone plugs anything in.

## The agent

`07_agent` ? frames: agent

Ask the agent. It reads this tour, calls the read-only tools and answers with the numbers: The Foundry, and a reduced look as the cheaper option. The tool trace is on the right. It proposes; it does not apply.

## The designer decides

`08_fix` ? frames: look-cut, studio-fixed, checks-pass

So the designer makes the decision: a seven-beam cut, nineteen point one kilowatts and three hundred and twenty-six kilos. Select it. The checks pass in both rooms. This is the loop the product is for — propose, check, decide, and the numbers follow the decision.

## The money and the proof

`09_money` ? frames: budget-lines, budget-over, budget-under

The money is not a separate spreadsheet. Add the real lines: rig and crew, five crew for two days, the van. The true total is twelve thousand seven hundred and forty-four. The first approval was twelve thousand — seven hundred and forty-four over. The producer approves thirteen thousand; the tour is two hundred and fifty-six under. Every look re-prices against that number.

## Sign the sheet

`10_sign` ? frames: cues-signed

Sign the safety cues. The sheet the crew gets is the sheet the checks were reading. Export CSV, HTML or JSON.

## Ready

`11_ready` ? frames: ready, home-final

From six empty fields to a tour that is checked, priced, signed and ready. The band and the rooms are invented; the arithmetic, the imports, the agent and the decisions are real. CUE designs; the crew runs the show.
