# The 90-second stage piece

Route: `/#/stage`. Deterministic, offline, sound on, reset with `R`, exit with `Esc`.

## Set

A black stage on screen, one practical lamp, headphones or studio monitors, and a printed cue sheet.
The screen is the previz. The room is the venue.

## The verb

Scrub the track and drag a look onto the build.

## Beat sheet

| Time | Beat | Action | Screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Cold open | One light, the intro, five days to the tour | Blackout, setlist, lamp |
| 0:10–0:25 | First touch | Scrub the track; the look rail and the cost follow | Previz, timeline |
| 0:25–0:45 | Agent works | Three looks, one video, visible flags | Proposals, rig, cues |
| 0:45–1:05 | Trust beat | The 12-fixture aerial is spectacular and unsafe; reject it | Warnings, glare cone, re-rank |
| 1:05–1:20 | Audience choice | Light drop or video drop; the room votes | Two drops, cost and complexity |
| 1:20–1:32 | Reveal | The show plays; the cue sheet prints; reset | Final 20 s, cue sheet |

## The trust beat

- The agent proposes **AERIAL 12** and ranks it first on impact (97).
- The previz shows the fan; the checks show the consequences:
  - **44.1 kW** against a **43.5 kW** supply;
  - **564 kg** on a **520 kg** front truss;
  - **glare 0.92** against a **0.55** limit.
- The designer rejects it. The reason is stored; the ranking recomputes.
- **SIX BEAM + MONOLITH** lands: impact 89, **34.5 kW**, **312 kg**, no audience-aimed fixture,
  **€1,373 under** the approved budget.
- The line: *"It was spectacular. It was wrong. The show is not the rig."*

The auto-reject at 0:58 is a fallback for a silent room, not the default. The button is the story.

## Audience agency

- **Light drop** — higher rig cost, 34.5 kW, content add-on.
- **Video drop** — cheaper rig, 19.9 kW, more cue complexity, and the `SIGNAL BARS` clip is missing.
- Both are defensible. The screen shows the trade-off. The presenter commits to the room's choice.

## Stagecraft

- One practical lamp for the cold open. Headphones for the track; monitors for the reveal.
- One low sub hit at the drop; silence under the trust beat.
- A printed cue sheet and a blackout cue. Hand it to someone in the front row after the reveal.
- Reset: `R`, under five seconds. The seed and the pack do not change.
- A backup laptop with the static build, and a 90-second screen recording as the last resort.

## Acceptance

- Five strangers: at least four can explain CUE in one sentence.
- First touch within 15 seconds without instruction.
- The drop lands by 30 seconds.
- The full arc runs in 92 seconds with the trust beat and the vote.
- Reset under five seconds.
- Two volunteers can change a look or vote without help.
- Runs with the network cable unplugged.
- Seven of ten viewers repeat the closing line.

The last two are measured in the room; the build gives them a fair chance.
