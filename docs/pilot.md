# One tour, 90 days

A pilot offer for one production company, one tour and one venue. Run beside the existing design
process, not instead of it.

## Who

- **Buyer:** the production company, not the freelance designer. The daily user is the production
  office; the designer is the author.
- **Pilot team:** one designer, one producer, one tour manager, one venue crew lead, one CUE engineer.
- **Scope:** three shows, one tour leg, one venue profile, one rights package, one budget.

## The 90 days

1. **Baseline (week 0).** Record the current process: previz iterations, cue-sheet changes, rehearsal
   hours, rig cost, approval time. No tool in the room yet.
2. **Load (week 1).** Import the show, the venue, the rig, the rights and the budget. Run in shadow
   mode. The production office does not act on CUE risks yet.
3. **Shadow (weeks 2–5).** CUE raises risks, the producer compares them to what the office already
   knows. No customer-facing alerts. Measure precision, recall and fatigue.
4. **Pilot (weeks 6–12).** Three shows with the desk in use. The designer uses the studio, the producer
   uses readiness, the crew uses the cue sheet. Safety remains human.
5. **Review (week 13).** Measure. Decide renew, extend or stop against the criteria written in week 0.

## What is measured

| Measure | Method | What it does not claim |
|---|---|---|
| Iterations to approval | count each previz review until the producer signs | not a claim about the quality of the idea |
| Cue changes in rehearsal | diff the cue sheet each day | not a claim that fewer cues is better |
| Rig cost avoided | the add-on removed before the order | not booked savings until the invoice |
| Rehearsal hours | crew timesheet, per show week | not a productivity score for a person |
| Risk precision | confirmed issues / risks raised | shadow mode only, per tour |
| Action conversion | risks that led to a rig, rights or content fix | not a claim about all risks |
| Approval time | version created → signed | not a substitute for judgement |

## Gates

- **Gate 1 — four-week shadow.** No customer-facing risk. Precision and fatigue measured. Fail if the
  producer cannot separate signal from noise.
- **Gate 2 — 90-day tour.** Precision ≥ 0.80, actionable rate ≥ 25%, no missed load-in. Fail if a
  rig, rights or power problem reaches the stage.
- **Gate 3 — production.** Two green months, a signed data-rights annex, and a security review.

## Kill criteria

Written before launch, not after:

- precision < 0.60 over a month;
- alert volume above the producer's weekly budget after dedupe and digest;
- any unsafe routing of a risk or a cue;
- any automatic release or automated safety sign-off;
- artist content leaving the production network without written permission.

## Commercial shape

- Per-show fee plus per-seat annual seats; the readiness layer is included in the tour tier.
- Setup/integration fee for the venue, previz and console adapters.
- Conversion credit from the pilot fee against the first annual contract.
- The public demo is free and is the top of the funnel; the pilot is the product.

## Demo numbers

The pilot dashboard uses synthetic numbers: 3 iterations vs 11 baseline, 4 cue changes vs 17, €8,640
rig cost avoided, 6.5 h rehearsal time saved, 0.86 precision, 0.31 actionable rate, 0.92 approvals on
time. They are labelled as synthetic in the app, the provenance sheet and this document.
